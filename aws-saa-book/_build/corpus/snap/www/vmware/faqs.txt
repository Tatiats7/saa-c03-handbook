# VMware Cloud on AWS
Migrate VMware Environments to AWS
## Notice
As of April 30, 2024, VMware Cloud on AWS is no longer resold by AWS or its channel partners. The service will continue to be available through Broadcom. We encourage you to reach out to your AWS representative for details. See FAQs below for more information.
## Benefits of VMware Cloud on AWS
VMware Cloud on AWS has transformed the industry by providing a scalable, secure & reliable solution to move VMware workloads onto the cloud. VMware Cloud on AWS is a managed service that combines AWS secure global cloud infrastructure with VMware SDDC software to deliver a fully supported, ready-to-run offering.
### Most extensive global cloud infrastructure
AWS spans 105 Availability Zones within 33 geographic regions around the world, providing you the cloud infrastructure where and when you need it.
### Most secure cloud computing environment
Supporting 143 security standards and compliance certifications, our core infrastructure is designed to meet the security requirements for the military, global banks, and other high-sensitivity organizations.
### Most functionality with over 200 services
AWS has continually expanded its services to support virtually any cloud workload, with more than 200 fully featured services for compute, storage, databases, networking, analytics, AI, and more.
### Largest community of customers and partners
AWS has an extensive global community, with millions of active customers and tens of thousands of partners globally

### FAQs

#### Is the VMware Cloud on AWS service still available?
Yes. The VMware Cloud on AWS service is still available to existing customers. Broadcom has indicated on their blog from May 6, 2024 that the service will “continue to serve customers just as it always has.”
#### What has changed with VMware Cloud on AWS?
Effective April 30, 2024, AWS can no longer resell the VMware Cloud on AWS service.
#### Will existing customers be able to purchase additional subscriptions from AWS?
Effective April 30, 2024, customers who purchased VMware Cloud on AWS through AWS will not be able to purchase new subscriptions, add-on services, or renew existing subscriptions.
#### Can existing customers buy additional subscriptions directly from Broadcom?
Broadcom has stated that customers can renew VMware Cloud on AWS subscriptions directly from Broadcom or an authorized Broadcom reseller.
#### How does this change impact existing customer billing?
Customers who have active one or three-year subscriptions including monthly payments that were purchased from AWS will continue to be invoiced by AWS until the end of their term. For any other questions related to billing, please reach out your AWS representative.
#### What does AWS recommend for customers seeking alternatives?
AWS and our partners can help you navigate your VMware Cloud on AWS strategy and help identify pathways, programs, incentives, and support if you are exploring alternatives.
