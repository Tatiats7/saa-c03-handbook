### Lab Maker
Lab Maker generates personalized, AI-powered labs in seconds based on what you want to build. Describe your learning goal in natural language, and Lab Maker creates step-by-step guided instructions overlaid directly in a simulated AWS Management Console. Practice hands-on with popular AWS services in a risk-free environment with adaptive AI guidance tailored to your needs. Available with a Skill Builder Team subscription.
### AWS Builder Labs
AWS Builder Labs give you over 200 guided, interactive experiences in safe AWS Console environments with step-by-step instructions to help you learn AWS services. Available with the AI-powered, trainer-inspired Learning Assistant to help you answer queries and explain code, all within your lab's context.
### AWS Jam
AWS Jam challenges you to apply your AWS knowledge to real-world scenarios that don't have step-by-step instructions. Choose your challenge level and technical domain, then use your knowledge to troubleshoot, debug, and solve open-ended problems in the AWS Console. You'll earn points, compete with colleagues through customized Jam Events with a Skill Builder Team subscription.
