## Why Skill Builder?
### Start or advance your cloud career
Whether you’re starting in the cloud or pursuing specialized roles in artificial intelligence, build the AWS skills that create opportunities. Learn at your own pace with digital training. Or, explore live in-person or virtual classes delivered by AWS or one of our global AWS Training Partners.
### Develop real-world skills
Get hands-on practice that prepares you for actual cloud roles. Build confidence through immersive AI-powered real-world scenarios that mirror day-to-day work. Upgrade to a Skill Builder Individual or Team subscription to access the full suite of immersive learning experiences.
Explore immersive learning experiences
### Showcase your expertise to employers
Show employers you're ready to deliver. Build confidence with certification exam prep, validate your practical skills, and showcase your achievements. Share your AWS achievements through Skills Profile. Display your certifications, badges, and hands-on accomplishments in one professional profile. Amplify your expertise on LinkedIn and expand your opportunities.

Build foundational to advanced concepts through self-paced and live training created by the teams who built AWS services:
Digital Courses (self-paced)

Accelerate your deep understanding of focused topic areas with self-paced instructor-delivered videos, hands-on labs, knowledge checks, and course assessments. Available with Individual Annual or Team subscriptions.
AWS Classroom Training (live)
Explore live classes with AWS-accredited instructors who teach through presentations, discussions, and hands-on labs. Ask questions, work through solutions in real-time, and get personalized feedback from experts with deep technical knowledge.
