# Lincoln Financial Transforms its Data Warehouse on AWS for Richer Business Insights
## Lincoln Financial Transforms its Data Warehouse on AWS for Richer Business Insights
Lincoln Financial struggled with an inefficient data warehouse for its sales professionals that was becoming more of a liability than an asset. To replace this warehouse, Lincoln Financial built a cloud-first data platform on Amazon Web Services (AWS) using services such as AWS Glue, AWS Lambda, and Amazon Simple Storage Service (Amazon S3). The new solution provides 360-degree views of sales professionals and their interactions, leading to richer business insights and better business decisions.
