## Smarsh and AWS empower businesses to thrive securely in the digital age
Smarsh, an Amazon Web Services (AWS) Partner, provides its clients with industry-leading technology that helps them to manage the risk and see the value in their communications data. In this video, Vinay Mehta, chief technology office (CTO) of Smarsh, shares how the company empowers the world's largest financial institutions with a homogeneous platform that transcends borders. Using innovative services like Amazon EC2, Amazon S3, AWS Key Management Service (AWS KMS), and Amazon Aurora, Smarsh delivers cutting-edge, scalable solutions that prioritize data protection and regulatory compliance.
