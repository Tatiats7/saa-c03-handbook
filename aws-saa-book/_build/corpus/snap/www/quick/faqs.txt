### General
Open all
#### What is Amazon Quick?
Amazon Quick is an AI assistant for work that turns questions into answers, answers into actions, and actions into outcomes—for you and your entire team. Quick connects with all your applications, tools, and data, creating your own personal knowledge graph that learns your priorities, preferences, and network. It doesn't just answer your questions, it knows how you want to work. Give it a task and it takes action - scheduling meetings, sending emails, creating dashboards, and following up on action items.
#### Who is Amazon Quick for?
Amazon Quick is built for anyone who works with information — no technical skills or coding experience required. It's designed to help people across every business function work faster:
- Sales: Pipeline tracking, customer research, call prep, and forecasting
- Finance: Report generation, budget analysis, invoice tracking, and financial modeling
- Marketing: Campaign performance analytics, content generation, and multi-tool data consolidation
- HR: Onboarding workflows, headcount planning, and employee communications
- Legal & Operations: Contract tracking, compliance workflows, and document analysis
- IT: Custom agent building, workflow automation, and system integration
Amazon Quick scales with your organization. Individuals and small teams can sign up for free—no AWS account, credit card, or IT involvement required. As your needs grow, Professional and Enterprise plans add corporate identity management, advanced governance, business intelligence with Quick Sight, and full AWS infrastructure connectivity for organizations of any size.
#### What can Amazon Quick help me with at work?
Amazon Quick can assist across a wide range of roles and functions, including sales, marketing, IT, operations, finance, and legal, by surfacing insights, automating workflows, creating custom applications, generating documents, and answering questions in natural language.
#### Can I use Quick with my existing tools, in my apps, and browsers?
Add Amazon Quick to the tools you already use and optimize your daily tasks with AI assistance. Whether you're browsing the web, collaborating in team channels, managing emails, or creating documents, Quick extensions bring AI-powered assistance directly into your existing workflows without requiring you to switch between applications. Add Quick extensions for Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, Slack, Microsoft Outlook, Microsoft Word, Microsoft Excel, Microsoft PowerPoint, and Microsoft Teams.

### Features and capabilities
Open all
#### What data can Amazon Quick access?
Amazon Quick can connect to your company's files, emails, documents, databases, data warehouses, and enterprise applications like Salesforce, Jira, and ServiceNow—as well as the public internet and third-party datasets. With Quick, your scattered data is securely unified in one place so you get better AI responses and spend less time searching.
#### Does Amazon Quick learn about my work over time?
Yes. Amazon Quick builds a personal knowledge graph that learns your role, priorities, relationships, and the way your work connects—so every answer and action is shaped by your context, not just your prompt. Quick compounds your knowledge over time. On desktop, the knowledge graph gets smarter every week as it learns the people, projects, and documents you work with. It's private to you, hosted on AWS, and meets the same enterprise security standards as everything else in your organization.
#### Can Amazon Quick automate my workflows?
Yes. Amazon Quick offers two levels of automation. Quick Flows lets any user build simple, repeatable workflows like generating a weekly report or routing approvals using natural language. Quick Automate handles complex, multi-step processes across systems, such as syncing data between Salesforce and your data warehouse or orchestrating cross-team operations. Both are AI-powered and require no coding.
#### Does Amazon Quick include business intelligence and data visualization?
Yes. Quick Sight, included within Amazon Quick Professional and Enterprise plans, delivers AI-powered business intelligence— interactive dashboards, pixel-perfect reports, embedded analytics, and natural-language data exploration. You can ask questions of your data in everyday language, create data stories that show what to do next, and share insights across your organization.
#### Can Amazon Quick conduct research and generate reports?
Yes, work that used to take weeks happens in minutes. Quick Research is a deep research agent that investigates your enterprise data, the public internet, and third-party datasets to deliver comprehensive, fully cited reports. From a simple prompt, it creates a research plan, gathers evidence, and produces an exportable report with a verifiable evidence trail.
#### How can my team collaborate in Amazon Quick?
Amazon Quick is built for teams. You can create Spaces—focused environments where your team shares knowledge, data, apps, and AI agent interactions across projects. You can also build custom chat agents tailored to specific tasks (like a customer service bot or a project management assistant) and share them with your team. Spaces and agents work together so your team's collective knowledge compounds over time.
#### Can I build custom applications in Amazon Quick?
Yes. Amazon Quick lets any user create interactive web applications in minutes using natural language—no coding or developer resources required. Describe what you need, and Quick generates a working application with live data connections that updates automatically. Whether it's a sales pipeline tracker pulling from Salesforce, a finance dashboard aggregating QuickBooks and Excel, or an operations monitor connected to your supply chain data, anyone can build and share the applications their team needs. Custom applications are currently available in preview.
#### What kinds of applications can I build with Amazon Quick?
You can build applications for virtually any business function. Sales teams create pipeline review and forecasting apps, finance teams build monthly close trackers and budget dashboards, marketing teams consolidate campaign performance across platforms, and operations teams monitor inventory and fulfillment in real time. Applications connect to your existing data sources, update automatically, and can be shared with your team—all from a simple prompt.
#### Can Amazon Quick create documents and visuals from a conversation?
Yes, work that used to require multiple tools and manual formatting happens in one place. Describe what you need in natural language, whether it's a report, a presentation, a spreadsheet, or a visual, and Quick handles the end-to-end creation process within your existing chat workflow. Iterate inline to refine your document until it's exactly right, then download polished files including Word documents, PDFs, PowerPoint presentations, Excel spreadsheets, and images, ready to share with your team or stakeholders.

### Plans and signup
Open all
#### Do I need an AWS account to use Amazon Quick?
No. You can sign up using your email, Google, Apple, Amazon, or GitHub account—no AWS account, no credit card, and no IT involvement required. Professional and Enterprise plans are available through the AWS Console for organizations that need SSO, region selection, and AWS infrastructure connectivity
#### Is there a free plan?
Yes. Amazon Quick Free gives you single-user access to most of Quick’s AI capabilities, including chat, Spaces, custom agents, knowledge bases, and extensions—at no cost. Every new signup also includes a free 30-day trial of the Plus plan with full features, including the desktop app and expanded agent hours. Get started here.
#### What’s the difference between Amazon Quick plans
Amazon Quick offers four plans—Free, Plus, Professional, and Enterprise. Free and Plus let you get started in minutes without an AWS account, while Professional and Enterprise add full business intelligence with Quick Sight, Quick Automate, AWS data source connectivity, corporate identity provider integration, and advanced governance. Visit the Amazon Quick pricing page for a full comparison.
#### Is my data used to train AI models?
No. AWS does not use your data to train models in any Amazon Quick plan—free or paid. Your data stays private.
#### How does the free trial work?
Every customer who signs up automatically gets a free 30-day trial of the Plus plan. During the trial, you get full access to Plus features—including the desktop app and expanded agent hours—and can invite up to 10 team members. After the trial, choose to continue with Plus or move to the Free plan.
