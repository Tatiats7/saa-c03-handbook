AWS Certification exam preparation and updates
Certification
- Coming Soon! AWS Certified CloudOps Engineer – Associate Exam: formerly known as AWS Certified SysOps Administrator – Associate, the Exam is being updated to align with the latest skills and knowledge in monitoring and maintaining AWS workloads. The updated exam, under its new name, will include containers as an in-scope topic. Registration opens on September 9, 2025. This exam is offered at $150 USD and will be available in English, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified Chinese. The last day to take the AWS Certified SysOps Administrator – Associate (SOA-C02) will be September 29, 2025.
Exam prep
Prepare for AWS Certification exams with confidence. Sign in to AWS Skill Builder to enroll in an Exam Prep Plan. Each plan includes free content such as practice question sets and enhanced content, via subscription, like official practice exams, self-paced labs, and game-based learning.
This month, we launched the following AWS Certification exam prep materials:
- AWS Escape Room: Exam Prep for AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03)
Looking for a fun way to get ready for your exam? Prepare to become an AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate in a 3D virtual escape room adventure. Complete a series of exam-style questions that include the answer rationales, build solutions in hands-on labs, and solve interactive puzzles developed by the experts at AWS.
AWS Escape Room: Exam Prep for AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03) is available within any AWS Skill Builder subscription and a free demo will be available in AWS Skill Builder soon. Learn more about preparing for AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03) on AWS Skill Builder.
