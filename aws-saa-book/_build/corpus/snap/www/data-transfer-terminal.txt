# AWS Data Transfer Terminal
Secure, physical locations for quick transfer of data to AWS
Get started with Data Transfer Terminal
## Why Data Transfer Terminal?
Providing a secure, upload-ready, physical location for you to bring your storage devices, AWS Data Transfer Terminal enables you to upload data to any AWS endpoint, including Amazon Simple Storage Service (Amazon S3), Amazon Elastic File System (Amazon EFS), or others, using a high-throughput connection.

## Benefits
Quickly transfer your data to the AWS cloud from a secure, physical location
### Use on demand
| Reserve a time slot at your nearest data transfer terminal, take your storage devices, and use a high-bandwidth connection to upload data to AWS. |
### Faster time to results
| Process and analyze ingested data within minutes, as opposed to days or weeks using traditional methods such as shipping devices. This rapid access to data enables immediate quality checks, crucial for time-sensitive and mission-critical applications. |
### Secure upload access
| Retain control of your devices throughout the process and rest assured that your data is safe. |
## Use cases
Find out how Data Transfer Terminal can help your business quickly and securely transfer your data to the cloud.
### Accelerate media production and delivery workflows
Efficiently transfer high-resolution audio and video files from locations without dedicated high-throughput connectivity, enabling seamless collaboration for geographically dispersed production and editing teams during post-production.
### Rapid automotive data processing
Upload massive sensor data from automotive test fleets to accelerate advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS) and autonomous driving development. Enable quicker processing and analysis of diverse driving scenarios, refining AI models for enhanced vehicle safety and performance.
### Efficiently transfer data created or collected in remote locations
Data Transfer Terminals are available where data is created or collected. This enables faster upload of Internet of Things (IoT) data from remote locations that support industrial, agricultural, or research and development use cases.
### Migrate legacy data
Securely upload legacy data to ensure compliance and easy data management. Maintain device custody throughout migration for data security.
