## About AWS Certification information and policies
AWS Certification helps you build credibility and confidence by validating your cloud skills with an industry-recognized credential. Explore the topics below to learn about what to expect with scheduling your exam, identification requirements, exam functionality, relevant policies, and more. Whether you’re considering taking your first AWS Certification exam or you’re ready to recertify your existing credential, it's important to be prepared.
