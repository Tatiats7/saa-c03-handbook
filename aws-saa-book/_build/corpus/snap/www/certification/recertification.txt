## Why keep your certification current?
To maintain your AWS Certified status, we require you to periodically demonstrate your continued expertise. Recertification helps strengthen the overall value of your AWS Certification and shows individuals and employers that your credential covers the latest AWS knowledge, skills, and best practices.
How to renew
How to maintain
### How to renew
Renew your certification for 3 more years of validity. Certification must be active to renew. Renewal can be done through multiple options - see full list of options for each exam on the tables below.
### How to maintain
Maintain your certification for 1 more year of validity. Certification must be active to maintain. See full list of options to maintain your certification on AWS Skill Builder.

## FAQs
### If I recertify, how is the new validity period added to the existing validity period?
For all recertification options—renewal and maintenance—time is extended based on the date of completion of that recertification action. For example, if you renewed by retaking the exam, three years are added from the date you completed that exam. Similarly, for maintenance, one year is added from the date you completed maintenance activities.
### What is AWS Cloud Quest: Recertify Cloud Practitioner?
AWS Cloud Quest: Recertify Cloud Practitioner allows certified individuals holding the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner to maintain active certification status. Certified individuals are eligible to complete AWS Cloud Quest: Recertify Cloud Practitioner when their AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner certification is due to expire within 6 months.
Completing this recertification option will extend the certification validity for 3 years. Sign in to AWS Skill Builder for more FAQs.
### Where can I find more Certification FAQs?
You can find more answers to certification questions here.
### What is required to maintain my certification?
You must have an active certification that is within 90 days of expiration and an active subscription to AWS Skill Builder (Monthly, Annual, or Team).
