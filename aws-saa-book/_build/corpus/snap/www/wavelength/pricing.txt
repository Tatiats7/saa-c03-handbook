## AWS services in Wavelength Zones
AWS Wavelength, in partnership with communications service providers, delivers on-demand compute and storage services to customers where they need them. AWS Wavelength enables customers to build and deploy applications that meet their data residency, low-latency, and resiliency requirements. AWS Wavelength offers the operational consistency, industry leading cloud security practices, and familiar tools for automation that are similar to an AWS Region.
Wavelength Zones are available across North America, Europe, Africa and Asia. Amazon EC2 instances and other AWS resources in Wavelength Zones will have different prices than in the parent region. EC2 instances are only offered on demand in Wavelength Zones. You can use your Instance Savings Plan on Wavelength Zones. Learn about the pricing for Amazon EC2 and Amazon EBS.
