### [Well-Architected Framework Review (WAFR)](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/userguide/wa-framework-review.md)

The Well-Architected Framework describes key concepts and design principles for creating and running workloads in the cloud.

### [Preparing for a WAFR](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/userguide/preparing-for-a-wafr.md)

It's important not to rush into running a Well-Architected Framework Review (WAFR) without sufficient preparation.

- [Workload and scope](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/userguide/workload-and-scope.md): A workload is more than cloud services or resources.
- [People and culture](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/userguide/people-and-culture.md): Every workload needs an owner, and many people and teams may be involved in the lifecycle of a workload.
- [Documentation and infrastructure](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/userguide/documentation-and-infrastructure.md): When running a WAFR, you may find that for many of the best practices you are following, you will have a documented process, standard operating procedure (SOP), runbook, or playbook.
- [Running a WAFR](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/userguide/running-a-wafr.md): After all the required preparation, it is time for you to run a Well-Architected Framework Review (WAFR).

### [Improving your workload](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/userguide/improving-your-workload.md)

At this point, you've prepared for the WAFR, completed a review, and assessed your workload against AWS best practices.

- [Identify and understand risks](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/userguide/identify-and-understand-risks.md): View identified risks as improvement opportunities.
- [Determine prescriptive solutions](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/userguide/determine-prescriptive-solutions.md): Once risks and improvement opportunities are understood in your organization's context, work with the teams on the mitigation.
- [Prioritize improvements](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/userguide/prioritize-improvements.md): No organization has unlimited time and resources.
- [Implement and track improvements](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/userguide/implement-and-track-improvements.md): The ideal outcome of successful implementations is a reduction in HRIs and MRIs, resulting in an improvement to the architectural health of your workload.
