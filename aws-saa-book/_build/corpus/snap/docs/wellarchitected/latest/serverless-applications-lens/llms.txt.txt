# Serverless Applications Lens AWS Well-Architected Framework

> This document describes the Serverless Applications Lens for the AWS Well-Architected Framework. The document covers common serverless applications scenarios and identifies key elements to verify that your workloads are architected according to best practices.

## [Definitions](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/definitions.md)

- [Compute layer](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/compute-layer.md): The compute layer of your workload manages requests from external systems, controlling access and verifying that requests are appropriately authorized.
- [Data layer](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/data-layer.md): The data layer of your workload manages persistent storage from within a system.
- [Messaging and streaming layer](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/messaging-and-streaming-layer.md): The messaging layer of your workload manages communications between components.
- [User management and identity layer](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/user-management-and-identity-layer.md): The user management and identity layer of your workload provides identity, authentication, and authorization for both external and internal customers of your workload’s interfaces.
- [Edge layer](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/edge-layer.md): The edge layer of your workload manages the presentation layer and connectivity to external customers.
- [Systems monitoring and deployment](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/systems-monitoring-and-deployment.md): The system monitoring layer of your workload manages system visibility through metrics and creates contextual awareness of how it operates and behaves over time.
- [Deployment approaches](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/deployment-approaches.md): A best practice for deployments in a microservice architecture is to ensure that a change does not break the service contract of the consumer.
- [Lambda version control](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/lambda-version-control.md): Like all software, maintaining versioning enables the quick visibility of previously functioning code as well as the ability to revert back to a previous version if a new deployment is unsuccessful.


## [Scenarios](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/scenarios.md)

- [RESTful microservices](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/restful-microservices.md): When building a microservice, you’re thinking about how a business context can be delivered as a re-usable service for your consumers.
- [Alexa skills](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/alexa-skills.md): The Alexa Skills Kit gives developers the ability to extend Alexa's capabilities by building natural and engaging voice and visual experiences.
- [Mobile backend](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/mobile-backend.md): Users increasingly expect their mobile applications to have a fast, consistent, and feature-rich user experience.
- [Streaming processing](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/streaming-processing.md): Ingesting and processing real-time streaming data requires scalability and low latency to support a variety of applications such as activity tracking, transaction order processing, click-stream analysis, data cleansing, metrics generation, log filtering, indexing, social media analysis, and IoT device data telemetry and metering.
- [Web application](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/web-application.md): Web application
- [Event-driven architectures](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/event-driven-architectures.md): Event-driven architectures are becoming a popular and preferable way of building large distributed microservice based applications.


## [Pillars of the Well-Architected Framework](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/the-pillars-of-the-well-architected-framework.md)

### [Operational excellence](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/operational-excellence-pillar.md)

The operational excellence pillar includes the ability to run and monitor systems to deliver business value and to continually improve supporting processes and procedures.

- [Organization](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/organization.md): Organization
- [Prepare](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/prepare.md): Prepare

### [Operate](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/operate.md)

Operate

- [Metrics and alerts](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/opex-metrics-and-alerts.md): It’s important to understand Amazon CloudWatch metrics and dimensions for every AWS service you intend to use so that you can put a plan in a place to assess its behavior and add custom metrics where you see fit.
- [Centralized and structured logging](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/opex-logging.md): Standardize your application logging to emit operational information about transactions, correlation identifiers, request identifiers across components, and business outcomes using structured logging.
- [Distributed tracing](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/opex-distributed-tracing.md): Similar to non-serverless applications, anomalies can occur at larger scale in distributed systems.
- [Prototyping](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/opex-prototyping.md): Use infrastructure as code to create temporary environments for new features that you want to prototype, and tear them down as you complete them.
- [Configuration](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/opex-configuration.md): For configuration management, use environment variables for infrequent changes, such as logging level and database connection strings.
- [Testing](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/opex-testing.md): Testing is commonly done through unit, integration, and acceptance tests.
- [Deploying](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/opex-deploying.md): Use infrastructure as code and version control to enable tracking of changes and releases.
- [Evolve](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/evolve.md): Evolve
- [Key AWS services](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/key-aws-services.md): Key AWS services for operational excellence include AWS Systems Manager Parameter Store, AWS Serverless Application Model, CloudWatch, AWS CodePipeline, AWS X-Ray, Lambda, and API Gateway.
- [Resources](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/resources.md): Refer to the following resources to learn more about our best practices for operational excellence.

### [Security](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/security-pillar.md)

The security pillar includes the ability to protect information, systems, and assets while delivering business value through risk assessments and mitigation strategies.

- [Identity and access management](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/identity-and-access-management.md): APIs are often targeted by attackers because of the operations that they can perform and the valuable data they can obtain.
- [Detective controls](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/detective-controls.md): Log management is an important part of a well-architected design for reasons ranging from security and forensics to regulatory or legal requirements.
- [Infrastructure protection](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/infrastructure-protection.md): For scenarios where your serverless application needs to interact with other components deployed in a virtual private cloud (VPC) or applications residing on-premises, it’s important to ensure that networking boundaries are considered.
- [Data protection](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/data-protection.md): Consider enabling API Gateway Access Logs and selectively choose only what you need, since the logs might contain sensitive data, depending on your serverless application design.
- [Incident response](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/incident-response.md): There are no security practices unique to serverless applications for incident response.
- [Key AWS services](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/key-aws-services-1.md): Key AWS services for security are Amazon Cognito, IAM, Lambda, CloudWatch Logs, AWS CloudTrail, AWS CodePipeline, Amazon S3, OpenSearch Service, DynamoDB, and Amazon Virtual Private Cloud (Amazon VPC).
- [Resources](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/resources-1.md): Refer to the following resources to learn more about our best practices for security.

### [Reliability](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/reliability-pillar.md)

The reliability pillar includes the ability of a system to recover from infrastructure or service disruptions, dynamically acquire computing resources to meet demand, and mitigate disruptions such as misconfigurations or transient network issues.

- [Foundations](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/rel-foundations.md): Foundations
- [Change management](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/change-management.md): Change management
- [Failure management](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/failure-management.md): Certain parts of a serverless application are dictated by asynchronous calls to various components in an event-driven fashion, such as by pub/sub and other patterns.
- [Limits](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/limits.md): In addition to what is covered in the Well-Architected Framework, consider reviewing limits for burst and spiky use cases.
- [Key AWS services](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/key-aws-services-2.md): Key AWS services for reliability are AWS Marketplace, Trusted Advisor, CloudWatch Logs, CloudWatch, API Gateway, Lambda, X-Ray, Step Functions, Amazon SQS, and Amazon SNS.
- [Resources](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/resources-2.md): Refer to the following resources to learn more about our best practices for reliability.

### [Performance efficiency](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/performance-efficiency.md)

The performance efficiency pillar focuses on the efficient use of computing resources to meet requirements and the maintenance of that efficiency as demand changes and technologies evolve.

### [Selection](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/selection.md)

Run performance tests on your serverless application using steady and burst rates.

- [Amazon API Gateway](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/amazon-api-gateway.md): To build RESTful APIs, use REST APIs from Amazon API Gateway.
- [AWS Lambda](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/aws-lambda.md): Provisioned concurrency initializes a requested number of execution environments so that they are prepared to respond immediately to your function's invocations.
- [AWS Step Functions](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/aws-step-functions.md): AWS Step Functions offers both Standard or Express Workflow types.

### [Optimize](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/optimize.md)

>As a serverless architecture grows organically, there are certain mechanisms that are commonly used across a variety of workload profiles.

- [Amazon API Gateway](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/amazon-api-gateway-2.md): Amazon API Gateway and AWS AppSync caching can be enabled to improve performance for applicable operations.
- [AWS Lambda](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/aws-lambda-2.md): Set your AWS Lambda function timeout a few seconds higher than the average execution to account for any transient issues in downstream services used in the communication path.
- [AWS Step Functions](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/aws-step-functions-2.md): AWS Step Functions monitor the Amazon CloudWatch metric ExecutionThrottled which reports throttling on state transition, the number of StateEntered events, and retries that have been throttled.
- [Review](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/review.md): There are no performance efficiency practices unique to serverless applications for this best practice.
- [Monitoring](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/monitoring.md): Monitor the Amazon CloudWatch AWS Lambda performance and concurrency metrics to understand performance details about a single invocation and the number of instances processing events across a function.
- [Tradeoffs](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/tradeoffs.md): Configuring AWS Lambda provisioned concurrency incurs charges to your AWS account.
- [Key AWS services](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/key-aws-services-3.md): Key AWS Services for performance efficiency are Amazon DynamoDB Accelerator, Amazon API Gateway, AWS Step Functions, Amazon VPC, NAT gateway and AWS Lambda.
- [Resources](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/resources-3.md): Refer to the following resources to learn more about our best practices for performance efficiency.

### [Cost optimization](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/cost-optimization.md)

The cost optimization pillar includes the continual process of refinement and improvement of a system over its entire lifecycle.

- [Cost-effective resources](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/cost-effective-resources.md): Serverless architectures are easier to manage in terms of correct resource allocation.
- [Matching supply and demand](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/matching-supply-and-demand.md): The AWS serverless architecture is designed to scale based on demand and as such there are no applicable practices to be followed.
- [Expenditure and usage awareness](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/expenditure-and-usage-awareness.md): As covered in the AWS Well-Architected Framework, the increased flexibility and agility that the cloud enables encourages innovation and fast-paced development and deployment.

### [Optimizing over time](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/optimizing-over-time.md)

See the AWS Well-Architected Framework whitepaper for best practices in the Optimizing Over Time area for cost optimization that apply to serverless applications.

- [Lambda cost and performance optimization](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/cost-and-performance-optimization.md): With Lambda, there are no servers to manage, it scales automatically, and you only pay for what you use.
- [Logging ingestion and storage](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/logging-ingestion-and-storage.md): AWS Lambda uses CloudWatch Logs to store the output of the executions to identify and troubleshoot problems on executions as well as monitoring the serverless application.
- [Leverage VPC endpoints](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/leverage-vpc-endpoints.md): If you use Amazon Virtual Private Cloud (Amazon VPC) to host your AWS resources, you can establish a connection between your VPC and serverless services like AWS Lambda and AWS Step functions.
- [DynamoDB on-demand and provisioned capacity](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/capacity.md): Often within your Serverless applications you will use DynamoDB, Amazon’s fully managed NoSQL database service.
- [AWS Step Functions Express Workflows](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/step-functions-workflows.md): When you are creating a workflow with AWS Step Functions you will be given 2 options for the type of workflow that you want to create: Standard or Express.
- [Direct integrations](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/direct-integrations.md): If your Lambda function is not performing custom logic while integrating with other AWS services, chances are that it may be unnecessary.
- [Code optimization](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/code-optimization.md): As covered in the performance pillar, optimizing your serverless application can effectively improve the value it produces per execution.
- [Resources](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/resources-4.md): Refer to the following resources to learn more about our best practices for cost optimization.
- [Sustainability](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/sustainability.md): The sustainability pillar includes the ability to continually improve sustainability impacts by reducing energy consumption and increasing efficiency across all components of a workload by maximizing the benefits from the provisioned resources and minimizing the total resources required.
