# Operational Excellence Pillar AWS Well-Architected Framework

> The focus of this paper is the operational excellence pillar of the AWS Well-Architected Framework. It provides guidance to help you apply best practices in the design, delivery, and maintenance of AWS workloads.

## [Organization](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/organization.md)

### [Organization priorities](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/organization-priorities.md)

Your teams need to have a shared understanding of your entire workload, their role in it, and shared business goals to set the priorities that will create business success.

- [OPS01-BP01 Evaluate external customer needs](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_priorities_ext_cust_needs.md): Involve key stakeholders, including business, development, and operations teams, to determine where to focus efforts on external customer needs.
- [OPS01-BP02 Evaluate internal customer needs](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_priorities_int_cust_needs.md): Involve key stakeholders, including business, development, and operations teams, when determining where to focus efforts on internal customer needs.
- [OPS01-BP03 Evaluate governance requirements](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_priorities_governance_reqs.md): Governance is the set of policies, rules, or frameworks that a company uses to achieve its business goals.
- [OPS01-BP04 Evaluate compliance requirements](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_priorities_compliance_reqs.md): Regulatory, industry, and internal compliance requirements are an important driver for defining your organization’s priorities.
- [OPS01-BP05 Evaluate threat landscape](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_priorities_eval_threat_landscape.md): Evaluate threats to the business (for example, competition, business risk and liabilities, operational risks, and information security threats) and maintain current information in a risk registry.
- [OPS01-BP06 Evaluate tradeoffs while managing benefits and risks](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_priorities_eval_tradeoffs.md): Competing interests from multiple parties can make it challenging to prioritize efforts, build capabilities, and deliver outcomes aligned with business strategies.

### [Operating model](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/operating-model.md)

Your teams must understand their part in achieving business outcomes.

### [Operating model 2 by 2 representations](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/operating-model-2-by-2-representations.md)

These operating model 2 by 2 representations are illustrations to help you understand the relationships between teams in your environment.

- [Fully separated operating model](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/fully-separated-operating-model.md): Applications refer to the workload serving a business outcome and can be custom developed or purchased software.
- [DevOps with cloud-managed service provider](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/devops-with-cloud-managed-service-provider.md): The DevOps with cloud-managed service provider model follows a you build it, you run it methodology for application teams.
- [Cloud operations and platform enablement (COPE)](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/cloud-operations-and-platform-enablement.md): This cloud operations and platform enablement (COPE) model seeks to establish a you build it, you run it methodology by supporting application teams to perform the engineering and operations activities for their workloads, adopting a DevOps culture.
- [Distributed DevOps](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/distributed-devops.md): The distributed DevOps model separates (or distributes) the application engineering operations and infrastructure engineering operations responsibilities across the engineering teams, following the COPE methodology.
- [Decentralized DevOps](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/decentralized-devops.md): The decentralized DevOps model is a variation of the you build it, you run it methodology where operations are primarily under the ownership of workload teams.
- [Evolving your operating model](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/evolving-your-ops-model.md): The models provided progressively move towards more autonomy at the workload level, matching the two-pizza team principle.

### [Relationships and ownership](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/relationships-and-ownership.md)

Your operating model defines the relationships between teams and supports identifiable ownership and responsibility.

- [OPS02-BP01 Resources have identified owners](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_ops_model_def_resource_owners.md): Resources for your workload must have identified owners for change control, troubleshooting, and other functions.
- [OPS02-BP02 Processes and procedures have identified owners](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_ops_model_def_proc_owners.md): Understand who has ownership of the definition of individual processes and procedures, why those specific process and procedures are used, and why that ownership exists.
- [OPS02-BP03 Operations activities have identified owners responsible for their performance](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_ops_model_def_activity_owners.md): Understand who has responsibility to perform specific activities on defined workloads and why that responsibility exists.
- [OPS02-BP04 Mechanisms exist to manage responsibilities and ownership](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_ops_model_def_responsibilities_ownership.md): Understand the responsibilities of your role and how you contribute to business outcomes, as this understanding informs the prioritization of your tasks and why your role is important.
- [OPS02-BP05 Mechanisms exist to request additions, changes, and exceptions](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_ops_model_req_add_chg_exception.md): You can make requests to owners of processes, procedures, and resources.
- [OPS02-BP06 Responsibilities between teams are predefined or negotiated](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_ops_model_def_neg_team_agreements.md): Have defined or negotiated agreements between teams describing how they work with and support each other (for example, response times, service level objectives, or service-level agreements).

### [Organizational culture](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/organizational-culture.md)

Provide support for your team members so they can be more effective in taking action and supporting your business outcome.

- [OPS03-BP01 Provide executive sponsorship](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_org_culture_executive_sponsor.md): Senior leadership clearly sets expectations for the organization and evaluates success.
- [OPS03-BP02 Team members are empowered to take action when outcomes are at risk](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_org_culture_team_emp_take_action.md): A cultural behavior of ownership instilled by leadership results in any employee feeling empowered to act on behalf of the entire company beyond their defined scope of role and accountability.
- [OPS03-BP03 Escalation is encouraged](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_org_culture_team_enc_escalation.md): Team members are encouraged by leadership to escalate issues and concerns to higher-level decision makers and stakeholders if they believe desired outcomes are at risk and expected standards are not met.
- [OPS03-BP04 Communications are timely, clear, and actionable](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_org_culture_effective_comms.md): Leadership is responsible for the creation of strong and effective communications, especially when the organization adopts new strategies, technologies, or ways of working.
- [OPS03-BP05 Experimentation is encouraged](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_org_culture_team_enc_experiment.md): Experimentation is a catalyst for turning new ideas into products and features.
- [OPS03-BP06 Team members are encouraged to maintain and grow their skill sets](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_org_culture_team_enc_learn.md): Teams must grow their skill sets to adopt new technologies, and to support changes in demand and responsibilities in support of your workloads.
- [OPS03-BP07 Resource teams appropriately](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_org_culture_team_res_appro.md): Provision the right amount of proficient team members, and provide tools and resources to support your workload needs.


## [Prepare](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/prepare.md)

### [Implement observability](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/implement-observability.md)

Implement observability in your workload so that you can understand its state and make data-driven decisions based on business requirements.

- [OPS04-BP01 Identify key performance indicators](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_observability_identify_kpis.md): Implementing observability in your workload starts with understanding its state and making data-driven decisions based on business requirements.
- [OPS04-BP02 Implement application telemetry](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_observability_application_telemetry.md): Application telemetry serves as the foundation for observability of your workload.
- [OPS04-BP03 Implement user experience telemetry](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_observability_customer_telemetry.md): Gaining deep insights into customer experiences and interactions with your application is crucial.
- [OPS04-BP04 Implement dependency telemetry](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_observability_dependency_telemetry.md): Dependency telemetry is essential for monitoring the health and performance of the external services and components your workload relies on.
- [OPS04-BP05 Implement distributed tracing](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_observability_dist_trace.md): Distributed tracing offers a way to monitor and visualize requests as they traverse through various components of a distributed system.

### [Design for operations](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/design-for-operations.md)

Adopt approaches that improve the flow of changes into production and that help refactoring, fast feedback on quality, and bug fixing.

- [OPS05-BP01 Use version control](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_dev_integ_version_control.md): Use version control to activate tracking of changes and releases.
- [OPS05-BP02 Test and validate changes](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_dev_integ_test_val_chg.md): Every change deployed must be tested to avoid errors in production.
- [OPS05-BP03 Use configuration management systems](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_dev_integ_conf_mgmt_sys.md): Use configuration management systems to make and track configuration changes.
- [OPS05-BP04 Use build and deployment management systems](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_dev_integ_build_mgmt_sys.md): Use build and deployment management systems.
- [OPS05-BP05 Perform patch management](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_dev_integ_patch_mgmt.md): Perform patch management to gain features, address issues, and remain compliant with governance.
- [OPS05-BP06 Share design standards](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_dev_integ_share_design_stds.md): Share best practices across teams to increase awareness and maximize the benefits of development efforts.
- [OPS05-BP07 Implement practices to improve code quality](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_dev_integ_code_quality.md): Implement practices to improve code quality and minimize defects.
- [OPS05-BP08 Use multiple environments](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_dev_integ_multi_env.md): Use multiple environments to experiment, develop, and test your workload.
- [OPS05-BP09 Make frequent, small, reversible changes](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_dev_integ_freq_sm_rev_chg.md): Frequent, small, and reversible changes reduce the scope and impact of a change.
- [OPS05-BP10 Fully automate integration and deployment](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_dev_integ_auto_integ_deploy.md): Automate build, deployment, and testing of the workload.

### [Mitigate deployment risks](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/mitigate-deployment-risks.md)

Adopt approaches that provide fast feedback on quality and provide rapid recovery from changes that do not have desired outcomes.

- [OPS06-BP01 Plan for unsuccessful changes](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_mit_deploy_risks_plan_for_unsucessful_changes.md): Plan to revert to a known good state, or remediate in the production environment if the deployment causes an undesired outcome.
- [OPS06-BP02 Test deployments](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_mit_deploy_risks_test_val_chg.md): Test release procedures in pre-production by using the same deployment configuration, security controls, steps, and procedures as in production.
- [OPS06-BP03 Employ safe deployment strategies](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_mit_deploy_risks_deploy_mgmt_sys.md): Safe production roll-outs control the flow of beneficial changes with an aim to minimize any perceived impact for customers from those changes.
- [OPS06-BP04 Automate testing and rollback](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_mit_deploy_risks_auto_testing_and_rollback.md): To increase the speed, reliability, and confidence of your deployment process, have a strategy for automated testing and rollback capabilities in pre-production and production environments.

### [Operational readiness and change management](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/operational-readiness.md)

Evaluate the operational readiness of your workload, processes, procedures, and personnel to understand the operational risks related to your workload.

- [OPS07-BP01 Ensure personnel capability](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_ready_to_support_personnel_capability.md): Have a mechanism to validate that you have the appropriate number of trained personnel to support the workload.
- [OPS07-BP02: Ensure a consistent review of operational readiness](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_ready_to_support_const_orr.md): Use Operational Readiness Reviews (ORRs) to validate that you can operate your workload.
- [OPS07-BP03 Use runbooks to perform procedures](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_ready_to_support_use_runbooks.md): A runbook is a documented process to achieve a specific outcome.
- [OPS07-BP04 Use playbooks to investigate issues](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_ready_to_support_use_playbooks.md): Playbooks are step-by-step guides used to investigate an incident.
- [OPS07-BP05 Make informed decisions to deploy systems and changes](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_ready_to_support_informed_deploy_decisions.md): Have processes in place for successful and unsuccessful changes to your workload.
- [OPS07-BP06 Create support plans for production workloads](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_ready_to_support_enable_support_plans.md): Create support for any software and services that your production workload relies on.


## [Operate](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/operate.md)

### [Utilizing workload observability](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/utilizing-workload-observability.md)

Ensure optimal workload health by leveraging observability.

- [OPS08-BP01 Analyze workload metrics](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_workload_observability_analyze_workload_metrics.md): After implementing application telemetry, regularly analyze the collected metrics.
- [OPS08-BP02 Analyze workload logs](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_workload_observability_analyze_workload_logs.md): Regularly analyzing workload logs is essential for gaining a deeper understanding of the operational aspects of your application.
- [OPS08-BP03 Analyze workload traces](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_workload_observability_analyze_workload_traces.md): Analyzing trace data is crucial for achieving a comprehensive view of an application's operational journey.
- [OPS08-BP04 Create actionable alerts](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_workload_observability_create_alerts.md): Promptly detecting and responding to deviations in your application's behavior is crucial.
- [OPS08-BP05 Create dashboards](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_workload_observability_create_dashboards.md): Dashboards are the human-centric view into the telemetry data of your workloads.

### [Understanding operational health](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/understanding-operational-health.md)

Define, capture, and analyze operations metrics to gain visibility to the activities of operations teams so that you can take appropriate action.

- [OPS09-BP01 Measure operations goals and KPIs with metrics](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_operations_health_measure_ops_goals_kpis.md): Obtain goals and KPIs that define operations success from your organization and determine metrics that reflect these.
- [OPS09-BP02 Communicate status and trends to ensure visibility into operation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_operations_health_communicate_status_trends.md): Knowing the state of your operations and its trending direction is necessary to identify when outcomes may be at risk, whether or not added work can be supported, or the effects that changes have had to your teams.
- [OPS09-BP03 Review operations metrics and prioritize improvement](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_operations_health_review_ops_metrics_prioritize_improvement.md): Setting aside dedicated time and resources for reviewing the state of operations ensures that serving the day-to-day line of business remains a priority.

### [Responding to events](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/responding-to-events.md)

You should anticipate operational events, both planned (for example, sales promotions, deployments, and failure tests) and unplanned (for example, surges in utilization and component failures).

- [OPS10-BP01 Use a process for event, incident, and problem management](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_event_response_event_incident_problem_process.md): The ability to efficiently manage events, incidents, and problems is key to maintaining workload health and performance.
- [OPS10-BP02 Have a process per alert](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_event_response_process_per_alert.md): Establishing a clear and defined process for each alert in your system is essential for effective and efficient incident management.
- [OPS10-BP03 Prioritize operational events based on business impact](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_event_response_prioritize_events.md): Responding promptly to operational events is critical, but not all events are equal.
- [OPS10-BP04 Define escalation paths](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_event_response_define_escalation_paths.md): Establish clear escalation paths within your incident response protocols to facilitate timely and effective action.
- [OPS10-BP05 Define a customer communication plan for service-impacting events](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_event_response_push_notify.md): Effective communication during service impacting events is critical to maintain trust and transparency with customers.
- [OPS10-BP06 Communicate status through dashboards](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_event_response_dashboards.md): Use dashboards as a strategic tool to convey real-time operational status and key metrics to different audiences, including internal technical teams, leadership, and customers.
- [OPS10-BP07 Automate responses to events](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_event_response_auto_event_response.md): Automating event responses is key for fast, consistent, and error-free operational handling.


## [Evolve](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/evolve.md)

### [Learn, share, and improve](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/learn-share-and-improve.md)

It’s essential that you regularly provide time for analysis of operations activities, analysis of failures, experimentation, and making improvements.

- [OPS11-BP01 Have a process for continuous improvement](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_evolve_ops_process_cont_imp.md): Evaluate your workload against internal and external architecture best practices.
- [OPS11-BP02 Perform post-incident analysis](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_evolve_ops_perform_rca_process.md): Review customer-impacting events and identify the contributing factors and preventative actions.
- [OPS11-BP03 Implement feedback loops](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_evolve_ops_feedback_loops.md): Feedback loops provide actionable insights that drive decision making.
- [OPS11-BP04 Perform knowledge management](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_evolve_ops_knowledge_management.md): Knowledge management helps team members find the information to perform their job.
- [OPS11-BP05 Define drivers for improvement](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_evolve_ops_drivers_for_imp.md): Identify drivers for improvement to help you evaluate and prioritize opportunities based on data and feedback loops.
- [OPS11-BP06 Validate insights](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_evolve_ops_validate_insights.md): Review your analysis results and responses with cross-functional teams and business owners.
- [OPS11-BP07 Perform operations metrics reviews](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_evolve_ops_metrics_review.md): Regularly perform retrospective analysis of operations metrics with cross-team participants from different areas of the business.
- [OPS11-BP08 Document and share lessons learned](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_evolve_ops_share_lessons_learned.md): Document and share lessons learned from the operations activities so that you can use them internally and across teams.
- [OPS11-BP09 Allocate time to make improvements](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/ops_evolve_ops_allocate_time_for_imp.md): Dedicate time and resources within your processes to make continuous incremental improvements possible.
