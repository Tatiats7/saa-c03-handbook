## [Cost optimization](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost-optimization.md)

- [Design principles](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/design-principles.md): Consider the following design principles for cost optimization.
- [Definition](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/definition.md): There are five focus areas for cost optimization in the cloud.


## [Practice Cloud Financial Management](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/practice-cloud-financial-management.md)

- [COST01-BP01 Establish ownership of cost optimization](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_cloud_financial_management_function.md): Create a team (Cloud Business Office, Cloud Center of Excellence, or FinOps) that is responsible for establishing and maintaining cost awareness across your organization.
- [COST01-BP02 Establish a partnership between finance and technology](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_cloud_financial_management_partnership.md): Involve finance and technology teams in cost and usage discussions at all stages of your cloud journey.
- [COST01-BP03 Establish cloud budgets and forecasts](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_cloud_financial_management_budget_forecast.md): Adjust existing organizational budgeting and forecasting processes to be compatible with the highly variable nature of cloud costs and usage.
- [COST01-BP04 Implement cost awareness in your organizational processes](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_cloud_financial_management_cost_awareness.md): Implement cost awareness, create transparency, and accountability of costs into new or existing processes that impact usage, and leverage existing processes for cost awareness.
- [COST01-BP05 Report and notify on cost optimization](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_cloud_financial_management_usage_report.md): Set up cloud budgets and configure mechanisms to detect anomalies in usage.
- [COST01-BP06 Monitor cost proactively](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_cloud_financial_management_proactive_process.md): Implement tooling and dashboards to monitor cost proactively for the workload.
- [COST01-BP07 Keep up-to-date with new service releases](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_cloud_financial_management_scheduled.md): Consult regularly with experts or AWS Partners to consider which services and features provide lower cost.
- [COST01-BP08 Create a cost-aware culture](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_cloud_financial_management_culture.md): Implement changes or programs across your organization to create a cost-aware culture.
- [COST01-BP09 Quantify business value from cost optimization](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_cloud_financial_management_quantify_value.md): Quantifying business value from cost optimization allows you to understand the entire set of benefits to your organization.


## [Expenditure and usage awareness](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/expenditure-and-usage-awareness.md)

### [Governance](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/governance.md)

To manage your costs in the cloud, you must manage your usage through the following governance areas.

- [COST02-BP01 Develop policies based on your organization requirements](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_govern_usage_policies.md): Develop policies that define how resources are managed by your organization and inspect them periodically.
- [COST02-BP02 Implement goals and targets](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_govern_usage_goal_target.md): Implement both cost and usage goals and targets for your workload.
- [COST02-BP03 Implement an account structure](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_govern_usage_account_structure.md): Implement a structure of accounts that maps to your organization.
- [COST02-BP04 Implement groups and roles](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_govern_usage_groups_roles.md): Implement groups and roles that align to your policies and control who can create, modify, or decommission instances and resources in each group.
- [COST02-BP05 Implement cost controls](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_govern_usage_controls.md): Implement controls based on organization policies and defined groups and roles.
- [COST02-BP06 Track project lifecycle](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_govern_usage_track_lifecycle.md): Track, measure, and audit the lifecycle of projects, teams, and environments to avoid using and paying for unnecessary resources.

### [Monitor cost and usage](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/monitor-cost-and-usage.md)

Enable teams to take action on their cost and usage through detailed visibility into the workload.

- [COST03-BP01 Configure detailed information sources](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_monitor_usage_detailed_source.md): Set up cost management and reporting tools for enhanced analysis and transparency of cost and usage data.
- [COST03-BP02 Add organization information to cost and usage](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_monitor_usage_org_information.md): Define a tagging schema based on your organization, workload attributes, and cost allocation categories so that you can filter and search for resources or monitor cost and usage in cost management tools.
- [COST03-BP03 Identify cost attribution categories](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_monitor_usage_define_attribution.md): Identify organization categories such as business units, departments or projects that could be used to allocate cost within your organization to the internal consuming entities.
- [COST03-BP04 Establish organization metrics](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_monitor_usage_define_kpi.md): Establish the organization metrics that are required for this workload.
- [COST03-BP05 Configure billing and cost management tools](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_monitor_usage_config_tools.md): Configure cost management tools that meet your organization's policies to manage and optimize cloud spending.
- [COST03-BP06 Allocate costs based on workload metrics](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_monitor_usage_allocate_outcome.md): Allocate the workload's costs based on usage metrics or business outcomes to measure workload cost efficiency.

### [Decommission resources](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/decommission-resources.md)

After you manage a list of projects, employees, and technology resources over time, you can identify resources that are no longer being used and projects that no longer have an owner.

- [COST04-BP01 Track resources over their lifetime](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_decomissioning_resources_track.md): Define and implement a method to track resources and their associations with systems over their lifetime.
- [COST04-BP02 Implement a decommissioning process](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_decomissioning_resources_implement_process.md): Implement a process to identify and decommission unused resources.
- [COST04-BP03 Decommission resources](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_decomissioning_resources_decommission.md): Decommission resources initiated by events such as periodic audits, or changes in usage.
- [COST04-BP04 Decommission resources automatically](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_decomissioning_resources_decomm_automated.md): Design your workload to gracefully handle resource termination as you identify and decommission non-critical resources, resources that are not required, or resources with low utilization.
- [COST04-BP05 Enforce data retention policies](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_decomissioning_resources_data_retention.md): Define data retention policies on supported resources to handle object deletion per your organizations’ requirements.


## [Cost effective resources](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost-effective-resources.md)

### [Evaluate cost when selecting services](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/evaluate-cost-when-selecting-services.md)

Best practices

- [COST05-BP01 Identify organization requirements for cost](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_select_service_requirements.md): Work with team members to define the balance between cost optimization and other pillars, such as performance and reliability, for this workload.
- [COST05-BP02 Analyze all components of the workload](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_select_service_analyze_all.md): Verify every workload component is analyzed, regardless of current size or current costs.
- [COST05-BP03 Perform a thorough analysis of each component](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_select_service_thorough_analysis.md): Look at overall cost to the organization of each component.
- [COST05-BP04 Select software with cost-effective licensing](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_select_service_licensing.md): Open-source software eliminates software licensing costs, which can contribute significant costs to workloads.
- [COST05-BP05 Select components of this workload to optimize cost in line with organization priorities](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_select_service_select_for_cost.md): Factor in cost when selecting all components for your workload.
- [COST05-BP06 Perform cost analysis for different usage over time](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_select_service_analyze_over_time.md): Workloads can change over time.

### [Select the correct resource type, size, and number](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/select-the-correct-resource-type-size-and-number.md)

By selecting the best resource type, size, and number of resources, you meet the technical requirements with the lowest cost resource.

- [COST06-BP01 Perform cost modeling](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_type_size_number_resources_cost_modeling.md): Identify organization requirements (such as business needs and existing commitments) and perform cost modeling (overall costs) of the workload and each of its components.
- [COST06-BP02 Select resource type, size, and number based on data](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_type_size_number_resources_data.md): Select resource size or type based on data about the workload and resource characteristics.
- [COST06-BP03 Select resource type, size, and number automatically based on metrics](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_type_size_number_resources_metrics.md): Use metrics from the currently running workload to select the right size and type to optimize for cost.
- [COST06-BP04 Consider using shared resources](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_type_size_number_resources_shared.md): For already-deployed services at the organization level for multiple business units, consider using shared resources to increase utilization and reduce total cost of ownership (TCO).

### [Select the best pricing model](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/select-the-best-pricing-model.md)

Select the best pricing model.

- [COST07-BP01 Perform pricing model analysis](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_pricing_model_analysis.md): Analyze each component of the workload.
- [COST07-BP02 Choose Regions based on cost](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_pricing_model_region_cost.md): Resource pricing may be different in each Region.
- [COST07-BP03 Select third-party agreements with cost-efficient terms](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_pricing_model_third_party.md): Cost efficient agreements and terms ensure the cost of these services scales with the benefits they provide.
- [COST07-BP04 Implement pricing models for all components of this workload](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_pricing_model_implement_models.md): Permanently running resources should utilize reserved capacity such as Savings Plans or Reserved Instances.
- [COST07-BP05 Perform pricing model analysis at the management account level](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_pricing_model_master_analysis.md): Check billing and cost management tools and see recommended discounts with commitments and reservations to perform regular analysis at the management account level.

### [Plan for data transfer](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/plan-for-data-transfer.md)

An advantage of the cloud is that it is a managed network service.

- [COST08-BP01 Perform data transfer modeling](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_data_transfer_modeling.md): Gather organization requirements and perform data transfer modeling of the workload and each of its components.
- [COST08-BP02 Select components to optimize data transfer cost](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_data_transfer_optimized_components.md): All components are selected, and architecture is designed to reduce data transfer costs.
- [COST08-BP03 Implement services to reduce data transfer costs](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_data_transfer_implement_services.md): Implement services to reduce data transfer.


## [Manage demand and supply resources](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/manage-demand-and-supply-resources.md)

- [COST09-BP01 Perform an analysis on the workload demand](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_manage_demand_resources_cost_analysis.md): Analyze the demand of the workload over time.
- [COST09-BP02 Implement a buffer or throttle to manage demand](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_manage_demand_resources_buffer_throttle.md): Buffering and throttling modify the demand on your workload, smoothing out any peaks.
- [COST09-BP03 Supply resources dynamically](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_manage_demand_resources_dynamic.md): Resources are provisioned in a planned manner.

Best practices

- [COST10-BP01 Develop a workload review process](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_evaluate_new_services_review_process.md): Develop a process that defines the criteria and process for workload review.
- [COST10-BP02 Review and analyze this workload regularly](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/cost_evaluate_new_services_review_workload.md): Existing workloads are regularly reviewed based on each defined process to find out if new services can be adopted, existing services can be replaced, or workloads can be re-architected.

Best practices
