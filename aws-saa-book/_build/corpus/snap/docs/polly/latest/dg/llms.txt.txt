## [Voices in Amazon Polly](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/polly/latest/dg/voices-in-polly.md)

- [Available voices](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/polly/latest/dg/available-voices.md): Amazon Polly provides a variety of lifelike voices in multiple languages for synthesizing speech from text.
- [Bilingual voices](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/polly/latest/dg/bilingual-voices.md): Amazon Polly has two ways of producing bilingual voices:
- [Applying the newscaster voice](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/polly/latest/dg/newscaster-voices.md): People use different speaking styles, depending on context.
- [Listening to voices](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/polly/latest/dg/listen-to-voices.md): Once you have set up Amazon Polly, you can test voices using custom text on the console.
- [Timing a voice speed](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/polly/latest/dg/voice-speed-vip.md): Because of the natural variation between voices, each available voice speaks at slightly different speeds.
- [Changing a voice speed](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/polly/latest/dg/voice-speed-change-vip.md): For certain applications, you may find that you'd prefer the voice you like be slowed down, or speeded up.
