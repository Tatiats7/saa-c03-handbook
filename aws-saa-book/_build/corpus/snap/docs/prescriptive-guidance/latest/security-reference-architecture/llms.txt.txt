## [SRA building blocks – AWS Organizations, accounts, and guardrails](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/security-reference-architecture/organizations.md)

- [Using AWS Organizations for security](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/security-reference-architecture/organizations-security.md): Description of the AWS Organizations service, foundational security capabilities and guardrails, and recommended multi-account strategy.
- [The management account, trusted access, and delegated administrators](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/security-reference-architecture/management-account.md): Describes AWS management and member accounts, their responsibilities, and their features.
- [Dedicated accounts structure](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/security-reference-architecture/dedicated-accounts.md): Describes the types of AWS accounts recommended for achieving effective security operations.
- [AWS organization and account structure of the AWS SRA](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/security-reference-architecture/account-structure.md): Describes the dedicated accounts and high-level structure of the AWS SRA without specific services.
- [Apply security services across your AWS organization](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/security-reference-architecture/security-services.md): The elements of an AWS environment, including organization, OU, account, network, principal, and resources.
