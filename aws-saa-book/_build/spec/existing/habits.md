## Habits and communication

I’ve been analyzing myself, my career, and the recent results of my interviews. I can clearly see that I have three main problems:

- Forgetting words, names, and terminology
- Struggling to articulate my thoughts clearly
- Being a very nervous person and therefore getting extremely stressed in stressful situations.

These problems affect my career a lot. Of course, if I’ve prepared an answer to the exact same question, I will likely answer it correctly, but even that is not guaranteed because I might still forget the words. And of course, you cannot predict all of their questions, ideas, directions.

It becomes even more difficult when I have to communicate in English, which is my second language. I struggle with the same problems even in my native language, Georgian, so you can imagine what happens in English, where my vocabulary is more limited and forgetting even one word has a much bigger impact.

I remember that in high school, whenever I had trouble expressing myself in Georgian, I used to read books, and I remember that it helped me a lot. Lately, I haven’t been reading books. Not only books, but I’m a bit ashamed to admit that I rarely read blogs as well.

I think I have a problem with processing context. I even get intimidated when LLMs generate a lot of text, and I often do not read all of it. When I need to do something I don’t know, instead of reading the documentation, I try to jump into action (coding) as quickly as possible. I’ve always been impatient, but I think this new world has made it even worse.

I’m also a bit lazy, and maybe a bit too reliant on being naturally intuitive. Sometimes just one quick glance gives me the general idea of the text, so my mind tells me, “Why read everything if you can understand the gist with almost no effort?” The same thing happens not only when I have to read, but also when I have to write prompts.

And as these models become more and more intelligent, I find myself trying less and less to be precise and understandable. Unfortunately, I think this is affecting not only my relationship with AI, but also my communication with people.

## Plan to improve this messiness

- Read 10 pages every day.  Books, blogs, documentation, long LLM reports, and papers all count. Aim for about 30 minutes daily.
- Take a 10-20 minute walk in the sun every day without looking at my phone.
- Write 0.5-1 pages every day:  even writing prompts counts. Aim for 10–15 minutes daily.
- Start taking magnesium to help with overall stress.
- Reduce scrolling:  define a clear rule for this and follow it consistently.
