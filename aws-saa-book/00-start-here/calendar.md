# Study calendar

## How to use this calendar (2 min)
<!-- section: role=orient; route=fast; minutes=2 -->

Day 1 is the day you start, and the days are calendar days in a row. Start with Plan A. Plan B moves the exam later, Plan C earlier, and the readiness gates say when to switch.

The exam takes [130 minutes](../facts/exam.md#exam-duration-minutes). [ESL +30](../facts/exam.md#exam-esl-accommodation) (English as a second language) adds a [30-minute exam extension](../facts/exam.md#exam-esl-accommodation). So train with [130 minutes](../facts/exam.md#exam-duration-minutes) and take the exam with 160.

Before you book, add five business days to your exam day: final results arrive [within five business days](../facts/exam.md#exam-results-timing). If that passes your deadline, read Plan C.

**Changed since the preview.**

- No dates, because your Day 1 is not fixed. The preview assumed Monday Oct 5.
- Plan C no longer promises a retake in October. Its retake rule now starts from your deadline.
- Plan B keeps Days 1 to 17 of Plan A, so it still works after Gate 2. About 48 hours, not 46.
- If you speak for colleagues before Day 8, do the Day 8 rehearsal the evening before.

A mock is one full Tutorials Dojo practice exam in timed mode, in one quiet block of [130 minutes](../facts/exam.md#exam-duration-minutes). Then review it for about 50 minutes, with the cause codes of `mistakes.md`. Pace: about 1 minute 45 seconds per question, which leaves about 15 minutes for questions marked with [Flag for Review](../facts/exam.md#exam-timer-and-review).

Answer every question. An unanswered question is [scored as incorrect](../facts/exam.md#exam-no-guessing-penalty), and there is [no penalty for guessing](../facts/exam.md#exam-no-guessing-penalty). The unscored questions are [not identified on the exam](../facts/exam.md#exam-unscored-questions-not-identified), so give every question the same care.

These are optional and not in the total:

- Speaking for 10 minutes each day: about 3 hours in all.
- The paid Official Practice Exam, where you [get a scaled score](../facts/exam.md#exam-official-practice-exam-scaled-score); Skill Builder [starts at $29 USD per month](../facts/exam.md#exam-skill-builder-individual-subscription).
- The tasks in `3-do.md`. Start with Task 0, the budget alert, in chapter 02.
- The Pass route for the Fast chapters of Days 11 and 13.
- The light chapters 20, 30 and 36, and 49 Speaking pack.
- The book's own mocks 50 and 51, and Anki.

Plan B has no gate: you take the exam on Day 23. A later move can be your third. To reschedule [a third time](../facts/exam.md#exam-reschedule-limit), you cancel the exam and schedule it again.

The cost is time. Final results arrive [within five business days](../facts/exam.md#exam-results-timing) after the exam. Most exams [do not display your pass/fail result at the end of the test](../facts/exam.md#exam-no-result-on-screen). Count five business days from Day 23: if that passes your deadline, Plan B does not fit.

A later exam also leaves less room for a retake. Check Plan B against your other deadlines of that week.

Plan C drops mocks 3 to 5, the repair day, chapter 44 and seven chapters of Plan A Day 13, such as 37 CloudWatch, CloudTrail.

Its gate is weaker: 70% or more on mock 1 and 75% or more on mock 2. <!-- no-fact: plan --> Decide after mock 2 on Day 11. If either mock missed, move the exam to Day 19. Do Plan A Days 13 to 17 on Days 12 to 16 and skip what you already did. Then take a light day, and do Plan A Days 18 and 19.

After a failed exam, you wait [14 calendar days](../facts/exam.md#exam-retake-wait) before a retake. After an exam on Day 13, the earliest retake is Day 27. Its result arrives [within five business days](../facts/exam.md#exam-results-timing). To fit a retake and its result before a deadline, book the first exam [14 calendar days](../facts/exam.md#exam-retake-wait) plus five business days ahead, or earlier.

> **Exam trap:** It is tempting to think that a failed early exam leaves time for a quick, free retake. You must wait [14 calendar days](../facts/exam.md#exam-retake-wait) before a retake. You also pay the [full registration fee for each exam attempt](../facts/exam.md#exam-retake-attempts-and-fee), [150 USD](../facts/exam.md#exam-cost) for this exam. <!-- trap: start-here-trap-quick-retake -->

## Readiness gates (3 min)
<!-- section: role=plan; route=fast; minutes=3 -->

Gate 1 comes after mock 3 on Day 14, Gate 2 after mock 5 on Day 17. Only first attempts in timed mode count.

<!-- table: plain -->
| Gate | If | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Gate 1 | Mocks 2 and 3 are both 80% or more <!-- no-fact: plan --> | You are ahead: mock 5 is optional. |
| Gate 1 | Mock 3 is under 65% <!-- no-fact: plan --> | Move the exam now: Plan B. |
| Gate 1 | Anything between | Continue Plan A. |
| Gate 2 | Last two mocks both 80% or more <!-- no-fact: plan --> | Go: the strict rule. |
| Gate 2 | Last two mocks 75% or more, no domain under 65%, 10 minutes left <!-- no-fact: plan --> | Go: the softer rule. |
| Gate 2 | Anything lower | Move the exam: Plan B. |

These percentages are the book's own rule, not an AWS threshold. The exam uses a [compensatory scoring model](../facts/exam.md#exam-compensatory-scoring): you need to [pass only the overall exam](../facts/exam.md#exam-compensatory-scoring), not each domain. So a weak domain alone does not fail you, but the softer rule keeps a floor of 65% for each domain. <!-- no-fact: plan -->

> **Exam trap:** It is tempting to read the passing score as a share of right answers. The passing score is [720 on a scale of 100 to 1,000](../facts/exam.md#exam-passing-score). Scores are [scaled scores, rather than the raw number of questions scored correct](../facts/exam.md#exam-scaled-scoring). So no third-party mock percentage converts to it. <!-- trap: start-here-trap-720-is-a-percentage -->

> **Exam trap:** It is tempting to move the exam late and often. You can reschedule [up to 24 hours before](../facts/exam.md#exam-reschedule-window) the exam time. Each appointment can [only be rescheduled twice](../facts/exam.md#exam-reschedule-limit). To change it [a third time](../facts/exam.md#exam-reschedule-limit), you cancel the exam and schedule it again. <!-- trap: start-here-trap-move-at-the-last-minute -->

So decide Gate 2 on the evening of Day 17. Each plan switch uses one of your two reschedules.
